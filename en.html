What we offer

Ecosystem solutions for your business

We unify messaging interfaces (Telegram / Viber) with backend database engines and interactive executive web panels to maximize workflow automation.

Logistics Bots for Couriers & Dispatchers

Smart bots for Telegram and Viber to completely automate courier/driver workflows: task assignment, photo confirmation of delivery notes, fuel logs, and instant issue reporting.

  • Step-by-step reporting in 2 clicks
  • Scans barcodes and invoice documents
  • Automatic GPS-tracking of delivery points

Management Analytics Dashboards

Interactive web control panels synchronizing seamlessly with messaging bots. Visualize your core KPIs, team efficiency, project budgets, and trip statuses in real time.

  • Profitability and utilization graphs
  • Integrates with BAS, KeyCRM, Google Sheets
  • Flexible user permission setup

Rental & Lease Automation

Automate car hire, heavy equipment rentals, bike sharing, or amusement parks toy cars. Tracks active lease sessions, calculates multi-tiered tariffs, and prevents operator theft.

  • Tracks rental terms, deposits, and collateral
  • Real-time session monitoring (parks, hotels, ports)
  • GPS tracking integration for equipment security

Intelligent B2B Lead Generator

AI-driven autonomous lead generation agents searching for clients, partners, and deals. Automatically scrapes job boards, public tender sites, and B2B Telegram channels.

  • Advanced AI-filters cleaning out spam and duplicates
  • Normalizes and cross-checks phone numbers and brands
  • Real-time export to Google Sheets and CRM
