2. Step-by-Step Operations

Step 1. 24/7 Source Monitoring

The system automatically scans designated open sources where prospective clients publish requirements:

  • Leading Job Boards: Analyzes corporate job descriptions to identify businesses hiring engineers, contractors, or developers for specific projects.
  • Specialized Platforms: Monotors construction orders, bidding registries, and vendor directories.
  • Messaging Channels: Scrapes targeted Telegram groups and channels where managers seek outsourcing partners.

Step 2. AI Filtering and Spam Removal

The bot does not just collect raw text—it filters data using semantic criteria:

  • Keyword Matching: Checks details against targeted fields (e.g., HVAC, electrical engineering, plumbing, ventilation).
  • B2B Profiling: Filters out private consumer requests and materials retail ads. The bot looks strictly for contract-level jobs, agencies, and subcontractors.

Step 3. Anti-Duplication Engine

Your sales pipeline stays clean thanks to cross-source checks:

  • Phone Normalization: Extracts all phone numbers and normalizes them. If a phone number is registered, the lead is discarded as a duplicate.
  • Brand Matching: Identifies company brand names. Even if a business posts across 10 sites under slightly different headings, it is logged only once.

Step 4. Auto-Cataloging & Instant Exports

Prospects are automatically exported to Google Sheets or CRM tabs sorted by categories:

  • 📐 Designers & Engineers (consultancies, CAD bureaus, planning offices).
  • 🔧 Installers & Contractors (HVAC installers, facility service providers).
  • 🏗 Developers & Builders (residential/commercial builders).

Simultaneously, the owner gets an instant message ping in Telegram with details and a direct URL link to the original listing.
