🚀 CarRent SaaS Case Presentation

🌟 Project Concept

CarRent is an out-of-the-box SaaS solution powered by a Telegram bot and cloud databases (Google Sheets, SQL API) built to fully digitize and automate the rental of kid toy cars in amusement parks and family venues.

Instead of relying on handwritten logs, messy cash operations, and dealing with constant employee theft risks, business owners receive a transparent, automated system where every dollar and every minute of rental is tracked in real time.

🛠️ Core Features (How the bot works)

The system is split into three access roles: Client, Operator (staff member at the venue), and Business Owner (Administrator).

1. Client Experience (Fast start, no queues)

  • Rent in 2 clicks via QR-code: Clients walk up to a toy car, scan the QR code sticker, and immediately load the Telegram WebApp interface.
  • Flexible rates: Options to select ride duration (e.g., 20, 40, or 60 minutes).
  • Frictionless payments:
    • Online card payments: Integrated with banks (Apple Pay, credit card, Mono).
    • Cash: Handed over to the operator and verified instantly via the bot.
  • Smart fallback logic: If mobile signal is low and card payment fails, the bot falls back seamlessly, offering to pay cash or change the order.
  • Time alerts: Customers receive a message in Telegram when their ride is ending, prompting them to extend the lease in 1 click.
  • Referrals program: Invite friends via referral link and earn credits/discount codes.

2. Operator Interface (Local control and notifications)

  • Instant notifications: The bot alerts park employees immediately when a client initializes a rental.
  • Cash verification button: For cash payments, operators tap a single button in their chat to log receipt of funds and trigger the ride timer.
  • Automatic tracking: The bot counts the minutes. The operator gets a ping: “Time is up for Toy Car #2. Please retrieve the vehicle or offer a lease extension.”

3. Business Owner (Global oversight)

  • Multi-venue management: Create new rental spots, add assets, assign workers.
  • Auto Google-Sheet (CarRent Sheet): Every transaction, ride status, cancellation, and amount is automatically exported to a cloud database.
  • Missed Revenue analysis: The bot logs instances where a client preferred card payment but paid cash or canceled due to lack of card support. This gives raw insights on cashless demand.

📈 Business Benefits (Why invest?)

Pain points without Bot Solutions with CarRent
Staff Theft: Workers pocket cash without logging rides in the paper ledger. 100% Tracking: A vehicle cannot be leased out undetected. The owner sees all active sessions and transactions live.
Accounting Chaos: Hard to tell which asset performs well and which sits idle or low on battery. Google Sheets Analytics: Automatic performance reports showing hourly revenues, venue metrics, and battery states.
Missed Sales: Parents have no cash, and there is no POS terminal at the venue. Instant Cards: Customers pay via cards / Apple Pay directly in the bot. No hardware terminals needed.
Human Errors: Staff forgets when a ride began, giving away free extra minutes. Auto Timers: The bot controls rides down to the second, notifying both client and operator.

💰 Financial Impact (ROI)

Implementing the CarRent bot enables:

  • 20-30% Revenue Growth by accepting card payments (parents rarely carry paper bills at parks).
  • Up to 15% revenue saved from being lost to dishonest staff actions.
  • Zero admin overhead — no need to physically visit venues to verify paper logs. The ledger is always in your pocket.

🚀 Customer Flow (Step-by-Step)

  • Step 1: Parent and child choose a toy car labeled: «Scan this QR code to ride».
  • Step 2: Scan code ➡️ enters bot ➡️ selects ride duration (e.g., 20 mins).
  • Step 3: Pay online or select cash.
    If cash: operator taps “Confirm receipt of 100 UAH” in their bot.
  • Step 4: Ride starts. In 20 minutes, bot notifies parent and staff.
  • Step 5: Data is logged automatically in the owner's Google Sheets.
